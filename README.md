## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Senior Software Engineer (Lead)** with **6 years of experience** designing, shipping, and scaling web and cloud-native applications. Today I lead a **five-person engineering team** delivering a **nine-product SaaS portfolio**, owning technical architecture, payment/PSP integrations, analytics infrastructure, and multi-environment DevOps.

I work end-to-end — from architecture and API design through payment systems, multi-tenant platforms, and analytics pipelines, all the way to production DevOps and incident response. Most of my recent work lives at the intersection of **payments, data, and reliability**: the parts of a product that have to be correct under load and observable when they aren't.

> **"Systems fail silently unless you design them to speak."**

---

## 🧭 Engineering Principles

- **Observability first.** If a system can't tell me what it's doing, it isn't done. I build metrics, tracing, and structured logging into the design — not after the incident.
- **Correctness over cleverness.** Payment flows, currency conversion, and tax logic don't get to be approximately right.
- **Boring infrastructure, interesting products.** Predictable, well-understood infrastructure is what lets the product surface be ambitious.
- **Security is a feature.** Secrets hygiene, CORS, JWT/cookie hardening, and dependency audits are part of "shipped," not a follow-up ticket.
- **Leverage, then delegate.** I mentor toward autonomy and use agentic tooling to compress diagnostics, review, and delivery.
